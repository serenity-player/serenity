## [Unreleased]

First public release (1.0.0). Date and compare link are added when the `v1.0.0` tag is created.

### Added
- Material You interface with wallpaper colors, tinted by the current album art.
- Bring-your-own-Client-ID onboarding and PKCE login with encrypted token storage and automatic refresh.
- Now Playing with play/pause, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat and volume.
- Spotify Connect device picker and playback transfer.
- Library: playlists, albums, liked songs and followed artists, with paging and pull-to-refresh.
- Search across songs, artists, albums and playlists; artist and album pages.
- Play from here, queue view and add to queue.
- Media notification, lock-screen controls and a home-screen widget.
- Adaptive layouts for tablets and foldables, predictive back, accessibility labels.
- Clear states for offline, rate limiting, expired sessions and accounts without Premium.
